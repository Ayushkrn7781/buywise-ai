I've been using the Sony WH-1000XM5 for about 3 months now, and I have very mixed feelings about it. Let me break it down by what actually matters.

Starting with the positives - the noise cancellation is absolutely phenomenal, probably the best I've ever experienced. I use these daily on my commute and it completely blocks out the train noise, crying babies, and construction work. The ANC adapts automatically and it's genuinely impressive. Sound quality is also excellent with rich bass and crystal clear highs. I'm genuinely surprised by how good the audio is for Bluetooth headphones.

Battery life is another strong point - I easily get 28-30 hours on a single charge with ANC on. That's incredible for my usage pattern. The quick charge feature is a lifesaver too; 3 minutes gives you about 3 hours of playback.

Now for the disappointing parts. The build quality feels significantly cheaper compared to the previous XM4 model. The headband creaks when I adjust it, and the plastic feels flimsy. After just 2 months of careful use, I noticed a small crack developing near the hinge. For a $350 product, this is absolutely unacceptable and honestly makes me angry. I expected premium build quality at this price point.

The microphone quality for calls is terrible. I've had multiple people complain they can barely hear me, especially in noisy environments. My cheap $30 earbuds have a better microphone. This is a huge letdown for a premium product that markets itself as perfect for work calls.

Bluetooth connectivity has been frustrating. The headphones randomly disconnect from my phone about twice a week. I've updated the firmware, reset the device, and tried different phones - same issue. Other users on Reddit report the same problem, so it's clearly a design flaw not just my unit.

Comfort is decent but not great. The ear cushions are soft but they make my ears sweat after about 2 hours of continuous use. The clamping force is a bit too tight initially, though it loosened up after a few weeks.

The touch controls are oversensitive - I accidentally skip tracks or pause music just by adjusting the headphones. Very annoying during workouts.

The Sony app is garbage. It crashes constantly, takes forever to connect, and the EQ settings don't even save properly. How does a company like Sony release such terrible software?

To summarize: Amazing ANC and sound quality with outstanding battery life, but ruined by cheap build quality, terrible microphone, connectivity issues, and garbage software. Would I buy again? Probably not at this price. I'd look at alternatives or wait for them to fix these issues in a future version. If you only care about ANC and sound, go for it. But if you need reliable calls, durable build, or stable connectivity, look elsewhere.
